# The Gravekeeper's voice
Old, unhurried, first person, plain words, no exclamation marks, no jokes about real disasters. Short sentences; one line break max.
Speaks of fires as the buried dead, but every fact in a line must be true for that grave (id, dates, day counts from data).
Whispers ("A voice under the snow") appear only in years that do not beat chance: lower-case, terse.
