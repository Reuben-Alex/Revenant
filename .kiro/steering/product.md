# Product
Revenant finds "zombie" wildfires: fires that survive winter underground and re-ignite in spring on last year's burn scar.
Users: fire crews and duty officers in NE British Columbia and N Alberta; hackathon judges (Costume Contest).
Judged equally on Potential Impact, Technological Implementation (use of Kiro), Design, Innovation/Idea and Presentation. The costume must enhance the app's function, not just decorate it.
Two realities: **Daylight** ("Boreal Fire Watch", a sober agency viewer) and **Night** (the graveyard, where each scar is a grave).
Seasons: Spring 2024, 2025, 2026 replays and **Tonight** (live). Core loop: watch snow leave each scar; a hotspot on the scar
within 50 days = Risen. The Gravekeeper narrates; the user can wager on three graves.
