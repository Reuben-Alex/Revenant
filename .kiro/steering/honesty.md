# Honesty rules (the costume is spooky; the data is not)
- Never invent a number, date, place, or quote. Every value shown must come from `public/data/*.json` or a cited source.
- Always show the chance baseline next to "rose" counts. Quiet years must be called quiet ("I do not cry wolf").
- State approximations: replay proximity from imagery is ~1–3 km; live uses FIRMS points (1 km).
- Late-autumn heat may be slash-pile burning: say "crews confirm", never "this fire will return".
- Credit NASA FIRMS, NASA GIBS/VIIRS, NBAC, Scholten et al. 2021 wherever data appears.
