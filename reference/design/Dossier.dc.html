What the satellite saw

NASA GIBS · VIIRS S-NPP corrected reflectance, true colour · ring marks the Apr 19 hotspot at 57.29°N 122.42°W

Why we say it rose

The holdover test from Scholten et al., Nature 2021, run on VIIRS 375 m detections and daily VIIRS snow cover.

  • On its 2023 scar. Hotspot inside the scar's ~3 km search band (the limit of the mapped imagery; the build uses FIRMS points for a true 1 km test).
  • Within 50 days of snowmelt. Snow left the scar Apr 8; heat returned 11 days later.
  • Not a gas flare. The spot is not one of the 320 points that burn in 3 of 4 years.
  • Lightning not checked. In mid-April a lightning start is unlikely, but Canadian lightning data isn't open, so this filter is off.

Snow on the scar, spring 2024

The teal band is the 50-day watch. A late snowfall briefly re-buried the scar on Apr 16–18; the heat came back the next day.
